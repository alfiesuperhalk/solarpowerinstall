Our Story

HE and Sons Corporation (HESC) traces its roots back more than five decades, when Henry Yu ventured into the fuel station business. Registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission on August 4, 1997, HESC has grown into a diversified corporate enterprise that carries on the family's entrepreneurial legacy.

Since launching its diversification strategy in 2000, HESC has expanded into growth sectors in step with the Philippine economy. Through strategic investments and sustainable growth, it continues to strengthen its portfolio, build resilience, and create long-term value across its businesses.

Vision and Mission

Vision. To be an integrated business entity that is profitable, reliable and dynamic in providing quality products and services to customers in Southeast Asia by 2040.

Mission. We build a happy and innovative team that provides excellent products and services to contribute to national growth and world development.

Corporate Governance

HE and Sons Corporation holds itself to high standards of corporate governance in decision-making and strategy, safeguarding the long-term interests of its shareholders. As a responsible corporate citizen, it keeps policies and programs in place so the company always does what is right in its everyday business.
